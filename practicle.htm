Patient check-up process

Steps to follow:

  1. Register at the reception.
  2. Share basic medical information.
  3. Meet the doctor for consultation.
  4. Complete the recommended tests.
  5. Collect the report and follow the advice.

Things to carry:

  • Identity card.
  • Previous medical reports.
  • Current prescriptions.
  • Appointment details.
